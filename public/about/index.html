Meet Elba Adams

Food writer · Mexico

Elba Adams shares approachable recipes for busy home cooks.

At The Everyday Table, she focuses on familiar ingredients, clear steps and practical tips that help make everyday meals easier.

About The Everyday Table

Each recipe aims to answer the questions that matter at the stove: what to buy, how long it takes, what to do in each step and how to store leftovers. The focus is on accessible ingredients and practical meals you can return to again.

Cooking times are estimates; appliances and ingredient sizes vary. Check that food is properly cooked and follow safe storage practices in your own kitchen.

How recipes are presented

Each page lists ingredients, numbered steps and practical notes on substitutions or storage. Photos illustrate the finished dish; your result may vary with ingredients and equipment.

How to use this site

Browse the recipe collection, choose a dish and follow the ingredients and numbered steps. Notes at the end of each recipe offer storage or preparation tips.
